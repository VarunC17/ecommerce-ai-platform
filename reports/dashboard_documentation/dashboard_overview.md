# Dashboard Overview

# ECOMMERCE-AI-PLATFORM Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

The **ECOMMERCE-AI-PLATFORM** dashboard is a production-style Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI Desktop** as the visualization layer and **PostgreSQL** as the backend data warehouse. It combines traditional business analytics with machine learning outputs and AI-generated business insights into a single interactive reporting platform.

The dashboard was built as the final component of the ECOMMERCE-AI-PLATFORM project, which includes data engineering, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, predictive analytics, recommendation systems, forecasting, and business intelligence.

The dashboard enables business users, analysts, and decision-makers to monitor overall business performance, understand customer behavior, evaluate predictive models, and identify strategic business opportunities.

---

# Dashboard Objectives

The primary objectives of the dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall business performance through executive KPIs.
- Analyze sales trends and operational performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and segmentation.
- Evaluate customer lifetime value (CLV).
- Identify customers at risk of churn using machine learning predictions.
- Present personalized product recommendations generated by the recommendation engine.
- Visualize historical sales and forecasting results.
- Provide AI-generated business insights and strategic recommendations.
- Deliver an interactive reporting experience for business stakeholders.

# Data Source

The dashboard is built using the **Brazilian Olist E-commerce Dataset**, which contains transactional e-commerce data including:

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Payments
- Reviews
- Products
- Sellers
- Geolocation

The raw dataset is processed through a complete ETL pipeline before being loaded into PostgreSQL and transformed into analytics-ready tables.

# Dashboard Pages

The dashboard contains seven interactive report pages.

## 1. Executive Overview

Provides an executive summary of the overall business including revenue, orders, customers, customer lifetime value, product performance, seller performance, and customer segmentation.

---

## 2. Sales Analytics

Analyzes historical sales performance, delivery metrics, payment behavior, product categories, seller performance, and operational efficiency.

---

## 3. Customer Analytics

Focuses on customer segmentation, RFM analysis, customer lifetime value, purchasing behavior, and geographic customer distribution.

---

## 4. Customer Churn

Displays machine learning churn predictions, customer risk levels, churn probabilities, and retention metrics to support proactive customer retention strategies.

---

## 5. Recommendation System

Visualizes personalized product recommendations generated by the recommendation engine, recommendation methods, recommendation scores, and customer-product recommendations.

---

## 6. Sales Forecasting

Presents historical sales trends, forecasting outputs, forecasting models, transaction trends, and future business projections.

---

## 7. AI Insights

Displays automatically generated business insights, strategic opportunities, business KPIs, and executive recommendations derived from the analytics pipeline.
