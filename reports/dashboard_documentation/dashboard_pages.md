# Dashboard Pages Documentation

# ECOMMERCE-AI-PLATFORM

## Dashboard Overview

The ECOMMERCE-AI-PLATFORM Power BI dashboard provides an end-to-end business intelligence solution built using the Brazilian Olist E-commerce dataset. The dashboard combines descriptive analytics, predictive analytics, machine learning outputs, recommendation systems, forecasting, and AI-generated business insights into a single interactive reporting platform.

The dashboard consists of seven report pages, each designed for a specific business purpose while maintaining a consistent visual style and user experience.

---

# Page 1 – Executive Overview

## Purpose

Provides executives and decision-makers with a high-level summary of overall business performance.

This page is intended to answer questions such as:

- How much revenue has the business generated?
- How many orders and customers exist?
- What is the average order value?
- Which products and sellers perform best?
- How are customers segmented?

## Main KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Customer Retention Rate
- Churn Rate
- Forecast Revenue
- Average Customer Lifetime Value

## Visuals

- Revenue Trend
- Orders Trend
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top Products
- Top Sellers
- Geographic Sales Distribution
- Customer Segment Distribution

## Slicers

- Date
- Product Category
- Customer Segment

## Business Value

Provides executives with a quick overview of overall business performance and highlights important trends without requiring detailed analysis.
